Celebrate the birth of our Saviour and share the joy and blessings of Christmas with the seniors.

## Programme

- Baptism
- Christmas worship
- Christmas message
- Room-to-room carolling

## Join the Carolling Team

We invite brothers and sisters who are willing to serve to join the carolling team, going from room to room to sing carols and share the joy of Christmas with the seniors.

## Registration

**Registration closes 15 November.** If you are interested, please sign up with Sister Jia Jia or Rev. Serene Ang.
